When to use it. Try a normal download first. Use this when yt-dlp doesn't recognise the site but the page plays audio in a browser.

How long it takes. Plain files download at full speed. A player that streams through Media Source has to play through, so that capture takes as long as the track. Preview tells you which it is.

Sites with a login. Run scripts/page-login.sh <url> once on the server. Your own servers on private addresses also need listing in PAGE_CAPTURE_ALLOW_HOSTS.

What it won't do. It doesn't capture DRM-protected or encrypted audio, and it doesn't get past bot checks or CAPTCHAs.

Check first. Only save audio you have the right to keep. Many streaming sites' terms forbid downloading, and this tool can't tell.

What it does. Adds a chapter called [SponsorBlock]: Sponsor (or Unpaid/Self Promotion) wherever viewers have flagged an ad read or a "subscribe to my channel" plug. In Plex you jump past it from the chapter list. The video itself is complete.

What it does to song chapters. If an ad sits in the middle of a song's chapter, that chapter is split around it: Battery → [SponsorBlock]: Sponsor → Battery. A video with no chapters gets three: title → Sponsor → title.

When to use it. Big channels that put sponsor reads inside the video (TV-style shows, podcasts). Most full-concert uploads have none, so it usually does nothing there.

No Skip button. Plex's automatic Skip buttons come only from its own detection, so this can't make one appear.

Online lookup. Each video is looked up on sponsor.ajay.app, sending only a short hash prefix of its ID. If the service is down the download still finishes, without marks. Marks are fixed at download time; later submissions aren't added.
