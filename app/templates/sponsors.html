Sponsor segments in the library

Looks up every downloaded video on SponsorBlock (using the YouTube ID in its .info.json) and lists the ones where viewers have flagged a sponsor read or self-promotion. Add marks writes those spots into the file as chapters, like [SponsorBlock]: Sponsor. Nothing is cut and nothing is re-downloaded. The file's original chapter list is saved first, so Undo puts it back exactly.

Plex can't show a Skip button for these. You jump past them from the chapter list. If an ad sits inside a song's chapter, that chapter is split around it.
